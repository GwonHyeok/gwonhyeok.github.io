Privacy Policy

Voxtonic — Offline AI Voice TTS · Effective date: May 21, 2026 · Last updated: May 21, 2026

The short version

Voxtonic runs text-to-speech entirely on your device. We (the developer) do not operate a backend server, do not have user accounts, and do not collect, sell, or share your text, voice recordings, or generated audio.

The only data leaving your device comes from third-party SDKs we embed for advertising (Google AdMob) and from the one-time download of the AI model files from Hugging Face. Details below.

1. Who we are

The "Voxtonic" Android application ("the App") is published by an independent developer, GwonHyeok ("we", "us", "developer"), operating under the brand GHPlanet. The App is powered by the open-source Supertonic 3 model released by Supertone, but this Privacy Policy covers only the Voxtonic Android application.

Contact: me@ghyeok.io

2. What we do not collect

We want to be explicit about this because it is unusual for a "free + ads" app to collect so little. The developer does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier.
  • The text you type into the App or any prompts you create.
  • The voice recordings you make as reference audio.
  • The audio files the App synthesizes.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, SMS, location, or device files outside the App's own sandbox.

None of the above ever leaves your device through any channel we control. The App does not contain any analytics SDK operated by us. The App has no "sign in" feature.

3. Data processed on your device only

Everything the App does with the data you provide stays inside the App's private storage on your device:

  • Prompt text and synthesized audio (WAV files) — saved to the App's private files directory so you can replay them from the Library tab. You can delete each item from the Library, or clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → Voxtonic → Storage → Clear data.
  • Voice reference recordings — when you record or import reference audio, it is stored in the App's private files directory and used only to compute a local "voice embedding" that the on-device model needs for synthesis.
  • Preferences — selected language, selected voice, and onboarding state, stored via Android SharedPreferences.
  • Model files — the AI model weights (see Section 5) are downloaded once and cached in the App's files directory.

5. Third-party services and data they may process

5.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC). To serve and measure these ads, the AdMob SDK collects information from your device. This typically includes:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / AAID, which you can reset or limit in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address.
  • Device information such as model, OS version, language, and time zone.
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, dismissals).

This data flows directly from your device to Google. We (the developer) do not receive raw event-level user data from AdMob; we only see aggregated reports.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's privacy policies and EU User Consent / CCPA programs. For details and to control or opt out of ad personalization, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob & AdSense data disclosure

5.2 Hugging Face (model file download)

On first launch, the App downloads the Supertonic 3 ONNX model files over HTTPS from huggingface.co. This request is necessary to make on-device synthesis work. The download server (Hugging Face) automatically sees standard HTTP request metadata such as your IP address and user agent. We do not attach any user identifier to this request.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob ad requests are configured with a neutral content rating; if you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App in a way that should be addressed, please contact us at me@ghyeok.io.

7. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA)

Because the developer does not store any personally identifiable information about you on our own systems, most data-subject requests (access, deletion, rectification, portability) cannot be fulfilled by us — there is simply no record on our side.

To delete the data stored on your device by the App, uninstall the App or go to Android Settings → Apps → Voxtonic → Storage → Clear data.

For data processed by Google AdMob, please exercise your rights directly with Google using the links in Section 5.1.

You can additionally limit ad personalization at the device level:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

8. International data transfers

The developer is based in the Republic of Korea. Because no personal data flows to our own infrastructure, no cross-border transfer occurs on our side. Third-party services (Google, Hugging Face) operate globally and may process data in countries other than your own; refer to their privacy policies for details.

9. Security

All data stays in the App's private sandbox, which Android isolates from other applications. Network requests to Hugging Face and Google use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; please contact us if you believe you have found a vulnerability.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change in a material way — for example, if a new third-party SDK is added or if a new permission is requested — we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
